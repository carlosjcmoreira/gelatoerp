---
name: Compras supply origins
description: Operational meaning of non-supplier purchasing labels and the safe identity rule for future catalogue work.
---

The purchasing spreadsheet's supplier column is not a reliable supplier registry. Matosinhos is an internal store that centralizes purchased stock and distributes it. Moedas is an operational purchasing category routed through Matosinhos for till change. Gráfica is an informal reference to the sign-producing company, not a confirmed legal supplier identity.

**Why:** Treating these labels as external suppliers creates false supplier records and unsafe invoice associations.

**How to apply:** Use stable store or supplier IDs. Represent internal hubs and operational categories as typed purchasing origins, and leave all other labels unresolved until a human confirms the canonical supplier. Preserve the raw label as evidence.
