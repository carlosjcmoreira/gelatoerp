---
name: Gelado production dispatch
description: Operational distinction between confirmed production allocated to Bolhão and manual dispatches from existing Matosinhos stock.
---

On production confirmation, the amount allocated to Bolhão is actual same-day dispatch, not a proposal awaiting later order creation. A manual dispatch from existing Matosinhos stock is additional; for example, 20 kg from confirmed production plus 5 kg from existing stock means 25 kg shipped.

**Why:** The user confirmed this as the operating model, including the additive treatment of manual stock sends.

**How to apply:** Keep production allocations and existing-stock dispatches as distinct, auditable origins. Do not subtract manual stock sends from the production allocation or count them twice.
